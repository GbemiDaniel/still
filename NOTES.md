# Notes

Status: Step 4 built plus the timer readability fix. Hosted on Vercel: project dees-projects-f5127dce/still, linked to GbemiDaniel/still, so every push to main deploys to production. Not yet tested on a real phone.

## What exists
- `src/haze.frag`: pass 1, slow warped fbm haze rendered into a small offscreen buffer.
- `src/field.frag`: pass 2 at full resolution: upsampled haze, the warm light (core, halo, wide falloff), vignette, filmic curve, one-step dither.
- `src/field.ts`: WebGL2 (WebGL1 fallback) setup, two passes, context loss handling.
- `src/breath.ts`: the breath controller. Pointer (any number of fingers), space or enter to hold; modes rest, in, out; one spring carries the value; idle breath resumes 7 s after a release.
- `src/main.ts`: governor level table, breath to shader, calm handling, type and hint switching.
- `src/grain.ts`: film grain as its own full device-resolution layer above the canvas.
- `src/engine/spring.ts`: now takes a damping ratio (zeta). zeta 1 keeps the exact closed form; below 1 uses substepped semi-implicit Euler.
- `src/engine/haptics.ts`: `tick()`: navigator.vibrate(8) on Android, the hidden `<input type="checkbox" switch>` click on iOS Safari 18+, nothing on desktop.
- `index.html`: canvas, the line (three phrases stacked in one grid cell so they cross-fade without shifting), the hint (a separate fixed element below the line, see LCP note), a CSS gradient fallback when WebGL is missing.

## Decisions
- Stack: Vite + TypeScript + raw WebGL, no 3D library. The whole piece is two full-screen fragment shaders, so a library would only add weight. JS is about 4 KB gzipped.
- Two-pass render: the haze is low-frequency, so it renders at half CSS resolution and is upsampled, while light and tone curve stay at the canvas resolution (native pixel ratio times the governor scale). A single full-resolution pass cost about 62 ms a frame at DPR 3 in the first perf audit.
- Field units: viewport height, capped at 1.6x width, so the light keeps its size on tall portrait phones (it filled 60% of the width at 390x844 without the cap).
- Colours: near-black with a cool violet cast (#050407 page background), light built from ember (1, .36, .10), amber (1, .60, .28) and a warm-white core (1, .90, .76), in linear light through a soft filmic curve. Warm against cool keeps the light the only warm thing on screen.
- Centring: the light sits at the exact centre of the viewport (uLight is always 0, core centroid measured at 0.00 px offset at rest, held, in landscape and back). Step 1 had a deliberate 0.07 field-unit lift to leave room for the type, plus a Lissajous drift that started rightwards (about 25 px right within 4 s on a 720 px-wide screen), and the breath lifted the light. All three are gone: the breath is purely radial and the haze carries the motion. A small symmetric wander was tried, but any drift starts in one direction and reads as off-centre, so there is none. The haze now tints the glow less (same average, half the swing) so its uneven shapes do not pull the glow sideways. The canvas is fixed to the visible viewport (inset 0), not 100svh, so its centre is the screen's centre whatever the browser bars do; resize and rotation are picked up every frame.
- Grain: moved out of the shader into `src/grain.ts`, because at governor scale 0.55 the shader grain was drawn at low resolution and stretched, which looked coarse. A 256 px triangular-noise tile is generated once and shown at one texel per device pixel (background-size 256/dpr, image-rendering pixelated). At 24 fps the layer moves by whole device pixels via transform, so renewing grain is compositor work: no shader cost, no repaint, never resampled. Strength 0.02 peak alpha, about 40% under the old 0.034. Dark specks at 0.6x, since they barely show on black and would grey the highlights. Calm holds it still. It fades in with the canvas and also sits over the no-WebGL fallback.
- Dither: the composite pass adds a static triangular dither of one 8-bit step, and the haze pass a half step before its 8-bit buffer. That breaks the long falloff into noise instead of bands, independent of the grain, and should also cure the calm-mode streaking.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond Light Italic, self-hosted via @fontsource (latin subset only, about 22 KB woff2). A quiet, high-contrast serif that reads as elegant at one line. Lowercase, slight letter spacing, warm off-white at 84%, placed 17svh above the bottom so the light owns the upper field.
- Copy: "breathe with the light" at rest, "breathe in" while held, "breathe out" after release, back to rest when idle resumes. Hint below it, "touch and hold" (coarse pointer) or "press and hold", fades in about 4 s after load and leaves for good on the first hold. It is the only instruction, so the piece still needs no explanation.
- Breath range: idle breathing stays in the lower 42% (IDLE_DEPTH), so a hold always has somewhere to go. The first visual QA showed holding looked nearly identical to rest because idle already used the full range. The shader range also widened: core radius 0.045 to 0.11, energy 0.7 to 1.4, wide falloff reaching across the field, haze warming up, vignette opening.
- Spring feel: inhale omega 1.3, zeta 0.9 (about 3 s to 90%). Exhale omega 0.7, zeta 0.72: longer than the inhale, as a real exhale is, settling with a soft ~4% give below rest. Idle tracking omega 2.5, critically damped. Calm: 0.75x speed and zeta 1, so nothing overshoots, and the breath maps to 0.15 to 0.8.
- Haptic: one 8 ms tick on release only (as briefed). A blur or tab switch mid-breath releases quietly, without a tick. iOS needs the call inside the pointerup handler, so it fires there synchronously.
- Long-press context menus are prevented and `touch-action: none` is on the page, so a held thumb never triggers selection, zoom or menus.
- Idle breath: 10 s cycle (about six breaths a minute, a common slow-breathing pace), cosine eased. Calm: 13 s, narrower range, 0.45x haze speed, still grain.
- Quality levels (governor table in `src/main.ts`, resolution from governor SCALES): L0 haze 0.5x CSS, 5 octaves, warp. L1 4 octaves. L2 haze 0.4x, 3 octaves. L3 haze 0.33x, no warp.
- No WebGL: a CSS radial gradient of the same light, so it still looks intentional.

## Measurements (local preview, 390x844 at DPR 3, 4x CPU, Fast 4G)
- Single pass: calibrated level 1, then 62 ms frames, dropped to level 3 (scale 0.55).
- Two pass: calibrated at 29.7 ms, start and settle at level 2 (scale 0.7), steady 60 fps after, worst frame 33 ms. LCP about 1.4 s, CLS 0.
- Visual QA at 390x844 and 1280x800: field near-black away from the light, clear amber falloff, no blockiness from upsampling, type reads well. Calm looks finished. Faint vertical streaking visible in calm only (still grain over 8-bit haze).

- Step 2 (local preview, 390x844): hold timeline measured from the real breath value at 60 fps: idle 0 to 0.41, held 0.62 at 1 s, 0.97 at 3 s, 1.00 at 4 s; brightness 0.2 field units from the light goes from 44 (rest trough) to 116 (held). Release eases down over about 4 s with a slight dip below rest, then idle resumes 7 s after release.
- Visual QA (step 2): held vs rest clearly different and premium, no blown-out core, no hard edges or banding, hint legible but quiet, calm hold still reads as a breath, no console errors.
- QA caveat: the Playwright window can be throttled to about 1 frame a second when occluded, and emulated reduced motion persists across reloads. Two early QA runs were invalid because of this. Always open a fresh page and confirm about 60 rAF a second first.
- LCP: with the hint inside the line's container, its fade repainted the line at about 5 s and Chrome counted that as LCP. With the hint as its own element, no late entry, the same as step 1. Perf audits that hold with synthetic `dispatchEvent` presses also keep LCP measuring while the phrases change (a real touch stops LCP measurement), so use trusted input (page.mouse or CDP Input) in audits.
- Perf (step 2, 4x CPU, DPR 3): audits were noisy. Calibration read 18.6 ms in one run (level 0, then stepped down to level 3 within 3 s) and 34.5 ms over only 28 frames in another (level 3). The hold only changes uniforms, so this is the same fill cost as step 1, not new work. CLS 0. TBT about 430 ms, mostly startup (shader compile).

- Fixes after step 2 (390x844, DPR 3): visual QA says the light is at exact centre and the glow is round and balanced; grain fine and subtle with no blockiness, including at ?quality=3; no banding in the glow at rest or held; ?quality=3 still premium; calm finished.
- Why a phone fell to level 3 in about 1.5 s: calibration decides after 30 ignored + 60 sampled frames, which is 1.5 s at 60 fps, and it used the mean. Load-time stalls inside that window (one measured run: a single 817 ms frame, avg 32.1 ms against a median of 16.7) drag the mean up. And intervals snap to vsync, so a phone that only just misses 16.7 ms reads 33.3 ms, which was past the 33 ms cut-off and went straight to level 3, skipping 1 and 2. Also, when early frames were very slow the time-based fade could end calibration with zero samples, and it then defaulted to 16.7 ms (full quality). Fix (small, in governor.ts): decide on the median, level 2 up to 34 ms, at least 20 samples unless 5 s pass, and no frames at all means level 3. The rolling watch is unchanged, so a genuinely slow phone still steps down within seconds. Three throttled runs (4x CPU) now all start at level 0 with median 16.7 ms and do not step down. The perf-auditor's Chrome ran this page at about 1.3 fps even unthrottled (Intel HD 520 with that browser instance), so it could not measure a phone; numbers above are from Playwright with CDP CPU throttling.

## Step 3 (modes, sound, offline)
- `src/content.ts`: every pace and every word of copy in one file. Paces (all longer out than in, 10 or 12 s a breath so 1, 3 and 5 min are whole breaths): easy 4/6, slow 5/7, long out 4/8. Lengths 60, 180, 300 s. I read "let me choose the paces and wording" as: I decide them, kept easy to edit here, and the person picks a pace and length on screen.
- `src/guide.ts`: guided timeline (3 s settle, then cosine-eased breaths, then done). Timed on the raw frame clock, so slow frames never stretch a session. `src/timer.ts`: three stopwatches (in, hold, out), durations only, nothing kept.
- `src/app.ts`: scenes (free, guided setup/run/end, timer setup/in/hold/out/result), the line (two spans cross-fading), the action row, the options sheet. `src/breath.ts` gained `enabled`, `press`, `letGo` and `drive` (a timeline blended in over 2 s, handed back to the spring at the end). Taps on buttons and sheets never start a hold.
- Guided end: the light settles, softens to 30% opacity (canvas and grain opacity only), closing line "rest here as long as you like", then again or done after about 2.6 s.
- Sound (`src/sound.ts`): synthesised open fifth plus pink-noise air, pad gain, lowpass and pitch follow the breath, about 25 parameter updates a second. Off at every load. `engine/audio.ts` no longer creates a context on the first tap anywhere; only the switch does, and it suspends the hardware when switched off or hidden.
- Keep awake (`src/wake.ts`): screen wake lock for the length of a session, on by default, switch hidden where unsupported. Nothing is stored anywhere, so toggles reset on reload by design.
- Calm: the `calm` class on the root slows UI fades; the light keeps its narrower range. Guided pace is unchanged in calm (the pace is the point).
- Notice: short line under the tabs on every non-running screen, full line plus privacy line in the options sheet. No medical claims anywhere in the copy; no em-dashes.
- Offline and install: `vite.config.ts` emits `sw.js` at build with the exact file list (version = hash of files and index.html), cache first, page refreshed in the background. `manifest.webmanifest`, icons from `scripts/make-icons.mjs`. Security headers (CSP allows only own origin) in `vercel.json` and in `vite preview`, so "nothing is sent" is enforced.
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond Light Italic for the voice (balanced wrapping, kerning, ligatures), Jost 400 for tools (tabular digits for the timer). The UI fades in after the font is ready so there is no swap.
- Governor: safe step up added. After a step down, a steady 60 fps (rolling average at most 17.5 ms) for 15 s tries one level up; if that slips within 10 s the wait doubles (max 120 s). Held during sessions through `canRaise`.
- Bundle: JS about 11.5 KB gzipped (was about 4), fonts 32 KB.

- Step 3 checks (preview build, 390x844, DPR 3, 4x CPU): no console or CSP errors; free, guided, timer and sheet flows run end to end; service worker activates; 6 requests, all same-origin. perf-auditor could not run (another Chrome holds the chrome-devtools profile), and the Playwright window was capped near 1 to 2 fps, so no valid LCP or frame numbers. One useful read: calibration measured a 16.7 ms median over 60 frames (level 0), then 4 s later a 35 ms average over 1 s took it straight to level 2. That is the very-slow rule (average over 33 ms for 1 s drops two levels) reacting to a throttled window, not to app cost, but it also shows how a single bad second at startup costs two levels. Visual QA's first pass was invalid for the same reason (transitions stalled at 2 fps); settled layout measured by DOM: no overlap at 390x844 or 360x640, touch targets 48 to 56 px.
- Why a mid-range phone drops levels fast: (1) calibration is only 1.5 s after load, exactly when shader compile, font decode and the first paints stall; (2) frame intervals snap to vsync, so just missing 16.7 ms reads 33 ms; (3) the very-slow rule drops two levels after one second over 33 ms; (4) before step 3 nothing ever stepped back up. Fixes so far: median decision, minimum samples, step-up probe with back-off. Open: consider requiring 2 s for the two-level drop, and ignoring the first 3 s after calibration.

- Debug readout (`src/engine/debug.ts`, shown in dev or with ?debug): moved to the top left, 9.5 px monospace, at most 46vw or 190 px wide (about 180x55 px at 390x844, was a block up to 60ch at the bottom left). Same fields: fps, ms, level (forced or not), scale, last change reason. It is a button marked data-ui, so the breath ignores taps on it; one tap collapses it to an 8 px dot coloured by level (green L0, yellow L1, amber L2, red L3) inside a 32 px tap target, another tap expands it. Collapsed state is not stored, and the text stops updating while collapsed. Checked at 390x844 (DOM and visual-qa): no clash with the gear, collapse and expand work, no breath on tap. The QA browser ran at about 1.5 fps, so its readout values were not meaningful.
- Testing caveat: the service worker serves the cached previous build first, so after a rebuild reload once before checking.

## Step 4 (pace that adapts to the person)
- `src/pace.ts`: Pace = in, hold, out, rest seconds, half-second steps. Ranges: in 2 to 10, hold 0 to 4 (capped low on purpose; optional, defaults to 0), out 2 to 14, rest 0 to 6. `suggestPace`: in = natural in x1.1 (at least +0.5 s), out = max(natural out, in) x1.15 and at least in + 1 s, rest 0.5, no hold. `guessNatural`: 0.8x in, 0.75x out when nobody has timed their breath.
- Presets (`src/content.ts`), starting points only, none holds: gentle 4/0/6/1, balanced 5/0/5/1, longer out 4/0/8/1. I kept "balanced" as equal in and out because that is what the name says; the other two lengthen the out-breath.
- Setup is one wide pace button (name or "yours", plus the summary), length chips, "find my pace" and "begin". The pace button opens a sheet (`src/paceEditor.ts`): presets, four steppers with 48 px buttons, "one breath takes N s", the dizzy line, find my pace, done. The setup panel stays the same height as step 3 so it clears the light on short phones.
- Find my pace (`src/find.ts`): hold to breathe in, let go to breathe out; a breath counts when the next press closes its out-breath; taps under 1 s and holds over 15 s (out over 20 s) are ignored; 4 breaths, median. Result scene shows the starting pace; "use this" saves it with the natural in and out.
- Ease-in (`src/guide.ts`): breath 1 is the natural rhythm (measured, or guessed); each breath moves one step towards the pace, reaching it after about a third of the session (2 to 8 breaths), smoothstep. Holding along is optional and never moves the light. Struggle = letting go before 60% of the in-breath (or 40% of a hold), or pressing again before 60% of the out-breath. A struggle in the last two breaths, or no touch for two breaths after someone had been holding along, holds the pace steady (no step that breath). Node test: plain 7.0 to 13.0 s over 5 breaths; struggle on breaths 3 and 4 holds at 9.1 s for three breaths then resumes; stopping at breath 4 holds at 12.4 s; slower at breath 2 applies from breath 3.
- Slower and faster: quiet buttons either side of "end", 6% a tap (0.7x to 1.4x), applied when the next breath starts, with a quiet note under the line. If the session ends adjusted, the scaled pace is saved (rounded to half seconds, so a single 6% tap on a short pace can round back to the same pace).
- Line words follow the phases (breathe in, hold, breathe out, rest) but skip phases under 1.5 s, which would only flicker.
- Remembered on the device (`src/store.ts`): localStorage key still.pace.v1 = pace, natural in and out, length. Saved on "done" in the editor, "use this", and an adjusted session; length changes save only once a pace has been saved. "forget my pace" in options removes it. All access is in try/catch, so private mode just forgets at the end of the visit. Privacy line now says the pace is kept on this device only; README updated (it used to say nothing is kept).
- Eyes closed: in a guided session the sound's pitch rise widens from 45 to 260 cents (a little over a whole tone) and tracks faster, so in and out can be heard; hold is a steady tone, rest a low one. Vibration switch in options (hidden without navigator.vibrate, so iPhone shows none): 16 ms as the breath turns in, 10-70-10 as it turns out. Off by default, like sound.
- Wording: dizzy line "If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, stop and breathe normally." shows in the notice on every guided scene except while running, in the pace sheet, and in options. The not-medical-advice line is unchanged. The timer is unchanged: durations only.
- Service worker fixes found while testing: (1) the version hash never included index.html (Vite adds it after my plugin ran), so a change only to the page, where all the CSS lives, would never reach installed copies; the plugin now runs post and fails the build if index.html is missing. (2) Install fetched through the HTTP cache and could store an older page; it now uses cache: 'reload'. (3) The page came from any cache and was refreshed in the background into the old cache, and old caches were deleted at once, so a page loaded during an update could ask for scripts already gone (a blank page, seen once). Now the page comes from its own version's cache, and old caches are removed 30 s after the new worker takes over. Tested old to new and back with a marker build: no errors, the update shows on the next load.
- Short phones: on guided scenes the notice is three lines (the dizzy line wraps), which pushed the setup panel to 19 px below the screen centre at 360x640, onto the light's core. A max-height 700px rule tightens gaps, the pace button and buttons to 50 px, tabs to 48 px and the notice to 12 px: the panel now starts 49 px below centre (step 3 had 56). 390x844 is unchanged (121 px clear). Disabled stepper buttons are at opacity .38 (they were .3): visibly off, still readable.
- visual-qa (390x844, browser at about 1 fps, so partial): no overflow, every target at least 48 px (steppers exactly 48), both notice lines present, pace sheet complete, options sheet fits, slower/end/faster 54 px tall, no console errors. It did not run the find-my-pace holds or confirm the slower note; I verified both in my own browser run.
- Checks (390x844, preview build): full flow in the browser: editor steps, saving, find my pace with real mouse holds (natural 2/3 s gave 2.5/3.5/0.5), use this, a session with slower, end, reload keeps the pace, forget clears it. No console errors after the service worker fixes. perf-auditor blocked again (chrome-devtools profile in use). Main-thread numbers from Playwright with 4x CPU over a 10 s guided session: 3.8 ms script per second, 0.45 layouts per second, one 63 ms long task (most likely the tap that starts the session), 3.2 MB heap. The window drew only a few frames a second, so these are a lower bound on the per-frame cost, not a phone measurement. The step adds no GPU work. JS is 14.9 KB gzipped (was 11.5).

## Timer readability fix (reported on mobile: timer hidden behind the light)
- Every time readout: (1) the live breath timer `#count` in timer mode (also used for the find-my-pace dots); (2) the timer result and found-pace values (`.result`, in the bottom panel over the scrim); (3) guided mode has no numeric countdown, only the 2 px progress hairline at the top; (4) "one breath takes N s" in the pace sheet (static); (5) the ?debug frame-time readout.
- Cause: `#count` was anchored to the line of text, `bottom: calc(var(--line-bottom) + 3.2em)`, and --line-bottom has a 164 px floor. On a 640 px tall screen that put the readout at 260 px from the bottom, just under the centre, inside the light's core; on taller phones it sat in the halo. It is only visible while breathing in and holding, which is exactly when the light is at its largest and brightest, and its warm off-white at 88% opacity sat on a warm-white glow. Measured at full breath (digits hidden, background captured, contrast of the text colour over every pixel): 360x640 worst 1.0:1 (median 2.8, 23 px from centre), 390x844 worst 3.5:1 (120 px away), 430x932 worst 4.4:1.
- Fix: the readout has its own band at the top centre, `top: safe-area-inset-top + 64px`, below the notch and the options button, clear of a thumb holding lower down and 196 to 334 px from the light's centre. Jost 400 at clamp(44px, 12vw, 60px), #fff6ec at full opacity, a faint text shadow, and a very soft radial dark backing (rgba of the page colour, .5 to 0, no edge) that is insurance more than necessity: the top of the screen is already near-black at full breath. Digits are tabular and padded with figure spaces to four characters, so the width never changes (checked: identical width at 0.x and 6.x s). Opacity fade only, so the calm version is the same.
- Accessibility: `#count` is role="timer" (aria-live off, so it is not read out ten times a second). The visible digits are aria-hidden; a hidden text span reads "breathing in, 6 seconds", refreshed once a second, and "2 of 4 breaths timed" during find my pace. Phase changes are still announced by the status region.
- The not-medical-advice line is now visible in every scene, including while a session runs (it used to fade out). The tabs and the session buttons share one grid cell (`#dock`), so the notice sits in flow below them instead of under an absolutely placed row. Also fixed on the way: "breathe out" wrapped to two lines beside "hold" at 390 and 360 (equal-width buttons were too narrow for it); buttons now size to their words and never wrap.
- After (full breath, 49 fps): worst-pixel contrast 11.9:1 at 390x844, 11.2:1 at 360x640, 11.9:1 at 430x932 (medians 13 to 14). All buttons one line; 6 to 7 px between buttons and notice. visual-qa at all three sizes: readable at full breath, clear of the gear, light unobstructed, no console errors; it found the first backing (.78) heavy, like a dark smudge, so it was softened to .5, and found the wrap and the tight notice, both fixed.
